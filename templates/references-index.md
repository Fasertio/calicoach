# References Index

Books, PDFs, courses and coach's notes you want the coach to follow. Drop files
in this folder and ask the coach to ingest them:

```
I want to follow <title>. The PDF is at calicoach/references/<file>.
```

The coach will read what you supply, extract only what changes programming, and
record where it agrees or conflicts with its defaults.

| Slug | Title | Author | Type | Read? | Primary for | Added |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Read?** is honest and matters: `yes` (text supplied and read), `partial`, or
`no — title only`. A source marked `no — title only` is never programmed from and
never paraphrased.

## How sources are used

- Your sources take precedence for **method and philosophy** — progression order,
  exercise preferences, technical standards, training style.
- They never override a screening contraindication, the load guardrails, or the
  red-flag referral rules. When they conflict, the coach shows both positions and
  the decision, and you choose within those bounds.
- **One primary source per block.** More than one governing method produces
  incoherent programming.

## Source notes

One note per source lives beside this file as `<slug>.md`.

## Privacy

Copyrighted books you own stay on your machine. `calicoach/.gitignore` excludes
PDFs and e-books from version control by default.
