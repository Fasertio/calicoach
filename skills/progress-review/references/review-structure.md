---

## Rules

- **Numbers, not impressions.** Every adherence and marker figure comes from the
  logs and the baseline file, not from memory.
- **Same standard, same conditions**, or the comparison is void — say so
  explicitly when they were not.
- **One honest cause per marker.** "Not sure" is a legitimate entry and is better
  than an invented explanation.
- **The MRV signal is the most valuable output of the whole review.** Do not skip
  it.
- **Maximum three changes** in the next-block brief. More than three and you
  cannot attribute the result to anything.
