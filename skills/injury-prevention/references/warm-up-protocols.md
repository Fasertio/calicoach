# Warm-up Protocols

8–12 minutes, every session. Structure: **pulse raiser → joint prep for today's
joints → specific ramp-up sets**.

Static stretching goes after training. Before training it produces a transient
loss of force and does nothing useful for injury risk.

---

## Universal opener (3–4 min)

| Drill | Dose |
|---|---|
| Easy cardio — bike, rope, brisk walk, jogging on the spot | 2–3 min |
| Arm circles forward and backward | 10 each |
| Trunk rotations, cat-cow, hip circles | 5–8 each |
| Deep squat hold with rocking | 30 s |

Aim for a light sweat and a raised breathing rate before any joint work.

---

## Shoulder prep (pull, push or overhead days)

| Drill | Dose | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Band pull-apart | 15 | scapular retractors, warms the mid-back |
| Band external rotation at the side | 12 each | cuff |
| Wall slides | 10 | upward rotation and thoracic extension |
| Scapular push-up (front support) | 10 | serratus |
| Scapular pull-up (hanging) | 5 with 2 s hold | scapular depression |
| Thoracic extension over a foam roller or a bench | 8 | if the overhead screen was limited |
| Sleeper-style / cross-body work | 30 s each | **only if internal rotation is limited**; skip with a shoulder-instability history |
| Dead hang | 20–30 s | tissue tolerance and grip |

## Elbow prep (any straight-arm or high-volume pulling day)

**Mandatory before planche, lever, back lever, one-arm or false-grip work.**

| Drill | Dose |
|---|---|
| Wrist circles and forearm shake-out | 30 s |
| Wrist curls, light | 15 |
| Reverse wrist curls, light | 15 |
| Pronation/supination with a light hammer or band | 12 each way |
| Elbow flexor isometric at 90° (light) | 2 x 20 s |
| Straight-arm plank, protracted | 20 s |
| Gradual planche lean / straight-arm load ramp | 3 x 10 s, increasing |

## Wrist prep (handstand, planche, floor pressing)

3–5 minutes. See also
[grip-and-forearm](../../exercise-library/references/grip-and-forearm.md).

| Drill | Dose |
|---|---|
| Wrist circles both directions | 10 each |
| Palm-down rocks, fingers forward | 10 |
| Palm-down rocks, fingers backward | 10 |
| Fingertip pulls (lift the palm, fingers stay down) | 10 |
| Side-to-side rocks | 10 each |
| Fist-to-palm transitions | 10 |
| Front support hold, protracted | 20–30 s |

## Hip and knee prep (leg days, pistol work)

| Drill | Dose |
|---|---|
| Leg swings, front-back and side-side | 10 each direction, each leg |
| 90/90 hip transitions | 8 each side |
| Deep squat hold with elbows pushing the knees out | 30 s |
| Glute bridge | 12 |
| Banded lateral walk | 10 steps each way |
| Bodyweight squats, progressively deeper | 10 |
| Ankle knee-to-wall rocks | 10 each |
| Calf raises | 12 |

## Lumbar and trunk prep (hinge days, lever days)

| Drill | Dose |
|---|---|
| Cat-cow | 8 |
| Bird dog | 6 each side |
| Dead bug | 8 each side |
| Glute bridge | 12 |
| Dowel hinge drill | 10 |
| Hollow hold | 20 s |

---

## Specific ramp-up sets

After joint prep, ramp the first working exercise:

| Working set | Ramp-up |
|---|---|
| Weighted pull-up 4x5 @ +20 kg | bodyweight x5 → +10 kg x3 → +15 kg x2 → work |
| Squat 4x6 @ 100 kg | bar x8 → 60 kg x5 → 80 kg x3 → 90 kg x1 → work |
| Tuck front lever 5x10 s | scapular pull-ups x5 → tuck hold x5 s x2 → work |
| Dips 4x8 bodyweight | support hold 20 s → 5 slow reps → work |
| Planche lean 5x12 s | front support 30 s → small lean 3x8 s → work |

Rule of thumb: **2–3 ramp-up sets**, each progressively closer to the working
load, none of them fatiguing.

---

## Cool-down (5 min)

| Element | Dose |
|---|---|
| Breathing down — nasal, slow exhale | 2 min |
| Static stretching for the day's tight structures | 30–45 s per position |
| Gentle mobility for the loaded joints | 2 min |

Static stretching after training is fine and helps with range goals. It is not a
recovery intervention and it does not prevent soreness — say so if asked.

---

## Time-pressed variants

| Available time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 12 min | full protocol as above |
| 8 min | 2 min pulse raiser + joint prep for today's joints + 2 ramp-up sets |
| 5 min | 1 min pulse raiser + the 4 most relevant joint drills + 2 ramp-up sets |
| < 5 min | cut the session's hardest exercise, not the warm-up |

The last row matters: an athlete who cannot warm up should train less, not warm
up less. Say it once and move on.

---

## Cold environments

Garages and outdoor bars in winter need **longer** prep — add 5 minutes, and add
2 extra ramp-up sets on anything straight-arm. Elbow and shoulder tendons in a
cold environment are the classic winter injury story in this sport.
