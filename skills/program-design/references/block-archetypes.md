# Block Archetypes

Pick one archetype per block. It sets volume, intensity, exercise selection and
what "progress" means. Secondary qualities are maintained, never developed
simultaneously.

Choosing rule: **the lowest applicable archetype wins.** New, returning,
constrained, or coming off a bad block -> Foundation, regardless of how strong
the athlete feels.

---

## 1. Foundation / GPP

**Who:** beginners, returners after 4+ weeks off, anyone with active
constraints, anyone whose screen produced 3+ Limited items.

**Length:** 4–8 weeks. **Aim:** tissue tolerance, technique, full range, base
volume, habit.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Frequency | 2–4 full-body sessions |
| Reps | 8–15, RIR 2–4 |
| Tempo | slow eccentrics (3–4 s) on most work |
| Intensity | never to failure |
| Straight-arm work | none, or scapular/tolerance work only |
| Skill work | positional only (hollow, support hold, wall handstand) |
| Prehab | heavy emphasis — 3 exercises per session |
| Progression | linear reps, then variation |

**Content pattern:** ring rows, incline/full push-ups, band-assisted or
eccentric pull-ups, dip negatives or box dips, goblet squats, RDL or hip thrust,
hollow and side plank progressions, full cuff and scapular circuit.

**Exit criteria:** 8+ strict pull-ups equivalent OR all screened items Pass,
technique consistent, no amber pain for 3 consecutive weeks.

---

## 2. Hypertrophy

**Who:** intermediates who need more muscle to make a skill or a load feasible;
athletes at a bodyweight where more tissue helps.

**Length:** 4–6 weeks. **Aim:** muscle cross-section.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Frequency | 3–5, upper/lower or PPL |
| Reps | 6–15, RIR 1–3 |
| Volume | upper end of the athlete's band, climbing ~1 set/pattern/week |
| Tempo | 2–3 s eccentric, controlled |
| Rest | 90–150 s |
| Weight room | heavily integrated — the best hypertrophy tool for lagging muscles |
| Skill work | maintenance only, 50% TUT, technique quality |
| Progression | double progression, then load/leverage |

**Content pattern:** weighted pull-ups and dips in the 6–10 range, ring rows and
cable rows, incline and flat pressing, dips, leg press or squat, RDL, leg curl,
lateral raise, curls and triceps work (yes — they are the limiting muscle for a
lot of calisthenics).

**Exit criteria:** planned bodyweight or measurement change achieved, or
strength markers up 10%+.

---

## 3. Strength

**Who:** intermediates and above with clean screens, chasing weighted
calisthenics or a heavier weight-room lift.

**Length:** 4–6 weeks. **Aim:** maximal force.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Frequency | 3–4 |
| Reps | 3–6, RIR 2–3, occasionally RIR 1 |
| Volume | moderate — quality over quantity |
| Tempo | controlled eccentric, intent-fast concentric |
| Rest | 180–300 s on primaries |
| Weight room | integrated; primary loading tool |
| Skill work | can coexist with straight-arm skills at low volume |
| Progression | load progression, 1–2.5 kg steps |

**Content pattern:** weighted pull-ups 4–5x4, weighted dips 4x5, barbell or
trap-bar hinge, squat, overhead or landmine press (constraint permitting),
heavy rows, loaded carries, direct grip work.

**Exit criteria:** target 5RM or 3RM achieved, or two consecutive weeks without
load progression.

---

## 4. Skill / straight-arm

**Who:** athletes with a clean elbow/shoulder screen, 8+ weeks of foundation
behind them, and a named skill target.

**Length:** 4–6 weeks. **Aim:** leverage progression on one primary skill.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Frequency | skill exposure 3–5x/week, short |
| Skill TUT | 120–240 s/week per skill, quality only |
| Reps | isometrics 5–12 s per rep, RPE 8, never to position failure |
| Bent-arm volume | reduced ~30% to make room |
| Rest | 120–180 s between skill sets |
| Prehab | elbow and wrist protocol mandatory, every session |
| Progression | leverage steps every 2–3 weeks, never improvised |

**Rules specific to this archetype:**
- **One primary skill.** A second skill is maintenance at 50% TUT.
- Straight-arm volume climbs at most 10%/week.
- Any elbow symptom cuts skill volume to zero for one week and restarts at 60%.
- Skill work is always first in the session, always fresh.

**Content pattern:** for planche — planche lean holds, tuck/adv-tuck holds,
pseudo planche push-ups, straight-arm pulldown, scapular protraction work. For
front lever — tuck/adv-tuck holds, ice-cream makers, straight-arm pulldown,
hollow-body, weighted pull-ups as the bent-arm base.

**Exit criteria:** the next leverage step held for the target time with clean
quality, or the block ends and you consolidate.

---

## 5. Peaking / expression

**Who:** athletes about to test, film, or compete a skill or a max.

**Length:** 2–3 weeks. **Aim:** express what is already built.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Volume | 50–60% of the previous block |
| Intensity | high, near-max attempts, but few |
| Frequency | maintained, sessions shorter |
| Accessories | minimal |
| Rest | long and generous |

**Exit criteria:** the test. Then deload, then a new block.

---

## 6. Deload / restoration

**Who:** everyone, every 4–6 weeks, or on signal.

**Length:** 1 week (occasionally 2 after a hard strength or skill block).

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Volume | 40–60% of the last hard week |
| Intensity | unchanged — keep the movement patterns sharp |
| Skill | technique only, 50% TUT |
| Prehab | full, unchanged |
| Conditioning | easy aerobic only |

A deload is not a week off and it is not a week of cardio. It is the same
program, smaller.

### The four levers — pick one or combine `[OG2]`

Deloading is not rigid. Manipulate whichever variable the block actually
fatigued:

| Lever | How | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Frequency** | drop two training days (4 days → 2) | general accumulated fatigue, life is busy |
| **Volume** | keep the schedule, cut half the exercises — usually the isometrics, keeping full-range work and skill practice | a skill or straight-arm block; connective tissue needs the break more than muscle does |
| **Intensity** | drop every progression back one level and keep the volume | joints and tendons are the complaint; the easier variation doubles as prehab |
| **Mixed** | fewer sets, longer rests, easier tempo, fewer exercises | when several things are tired at once |

**The most common practice** `[OG2]`: train only one or two days that week, one
exercise from each of push, pull and legs, one or two sets not to failure with
the last set taken to failure. That preserves strength while keeping volume low.
Spend the rest of the week on mobility, prehab and whatever the block neglected.

Then **test the new maxima at the end of the deload week**, one rest day before
the next block — see
[assessment-testing](../../assessment-testing/SKILL.md).

**Signals to deload early:** performance down two sessions running, resting heart
rate elevated, sleep degraded, persistent joint ache, motivation collapse,
unusual clumsiness in skill work.

## 7. Return-to-training (post-layoff or post-injury clearance)

**Who:** anyone back after 3+ weeks off, or newly cleared by a clinician.

**Length:** 2–4 weeks before rejoining a normal archetype.

| Variable | Prescription |
|---|---|
| Volume | start at 50–60% of pre-layoff, +15%/week |
| Leverage | regress one full step below where they stopped |
| Intensity | RIR 3–4 |
| Straight-arm | last thing to return, after 2 clean weeks |
| Monitoring | daily 0–10 joint check for the affected region |

Strength returns faster than tendon tolerance. That gap is where re-injury
happens. Regress leverage even when the athlete feels strong — especially then.

---

## Sequencing archetypes

Typical yearly shapes:

**Skill-focused athlete**
`Foundation (8) -> Hypertrophy (5) -> Skill (6) -> Deload -> Strength (5) -> Skill (6) -> Peak (2)`

**General strength / physique**
`Foundation (6) -> Hypertrophy (6) -> Strength (5) -> Deload -> Hypertrophy (6) -> Strength (5)`

**Constrained athlete rebuilding**
`Return-to-training (4) -> Foundation (8) -> re-screen -> Hypertrophy (5)`

Never run two consecutive blocks of the same high-fatigue archetype without a
deload between them.
