# Equipment Checklist

Confirm item by item. "I have a gym" is not an answer. For each item record
availability, the detail that matters, and whether safety was verified.

---

## Core calisthenics

| Item | Detail to confirm | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Pull-up bar | fixed / doorway / free-standing; height above head; grip diameter; can you hang fully extended? | doorway bars are the #1 anchor failure; a bar you cannot hang free from blocks leg-raises, muscle-ups and dead hangs |
| Gymnastic rings | strap length; anchor type and rated load; max height; can you go to ring-support and to floor-level? | rings unlock rows, RTO dips, levers, ring flies — and add stability load |
| Dip bars / parallel bars | width (should be ~shoulder width), height, stability | too-wide dips are a shoulder risk; low bars enable lever and planche work |
| Parallettes | height (low ~15 cm vs high ~35 cm) | high parallettes protect the wrist and enable L-sit/planche progressions |
| Floor space | length x width, ceiling height | handstands need ~2.6 m+; muscle-ups need headroom above the bar |
| Wall | free wall with clear floor | wall handstands, wall slides, chest-to-wall work |
| Mat / soft surface | thickness | bail practice, floor work |
| Resistance bands | thicknesses available (e.g. 15/25/35 kg equivalents) | assistance, prehab, band-resisted work |
| Weight vest / dip belt | max load, increments | weighted progressions |
| Ab wheel | — | anti-extension progression |
| Suspension trainer (TRX) | anchor height | row and fallout regressions when no rings |

## Weight room

| Item | Detail to confirm | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Barbell + plates | bar weight, smallest plate increment (fractional plates?) | progression granularity |
| Squat rack / power rack | safeties or pins? | training alone with a barbell requires them |
| Bench | flat / adjustable | pressing variations |
| Dumbbells | range and increments | unilateral work, prehab, substitutes |
| Kettlebells | weights available | hinge, carries, conditioning |
| Cable machine / lat pulldown | attachments available | straight-arm pulldowns are the best planche/lever accessory |
| Leg press / hack squat | — | knee-dominant options when squatting is contraindicated |
| Leg curl / leg extension | — | hamstring and knee support work |
| Back extension / GHD / RDL space | — | posterior chain, essential for lever and back health |
| Landmine attachment | — | shoulder-friendly pressing |
| Trap bar | — | hinge with less lumbar demand |
| Cardio equipment | which, and is it usable? | conditioning and low-impact options |

## Environment and safety

| Check | Question |
|---|---|
| Anchor rating | Is the bar/ring anchor rated for **dynamic** load, not just static bodyweight? Bolted into structure, not plasterboard? |
| Headroom | Can you press to handstand and do a muscle-up without hitting anything? |
| Fall path | Is the area around the bar and the handstand wall clear of glass, corners, furniture? |
| Flooring | Hard floor is fine for handstands, bad for high-volume jumping. Any mats? |
| Ring straps | Checked before every session for wear and correct buckle threading? |
| Training alone | If yes: no true failure on overhead barbell work; use safeties, dumbbells or machines for anything that could pin you. |
| Temperature | Cold garages need longer warm-ups, especially for elbows and shoulders. |
| Noise / neighbours | Constrains jumping, dropping weights, and sometimes training times. |

## Common substitutions when something is missing

| Missing | Substitute | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rings | TRX, low bar, or towel over a bar for rows | TRX cannot do RTO dips or levers safely |
| Dip bars | rings at hip height, or bench dips (last resort — worse shoulder position) | prefer parallettes on boxes over bench dips |
| Weight vest / belt | backpack with plates or books, or a dumbbell between the feet | check the backpack does not shift mid-set |
| Barbell | dumbbells, kettlebells, or heavy backpack | unilateral work covers most of the gap |
| Lat pulldown for straight-arm work | banded straight-arm pulldown, or tuck-lever pulls | band tension curve differs — cue the end range |
| Squat rack | goblet squat, split squat, step-up, sissy squat | knee-dominant volume is fully coverable without a rack |
| Leg curl | Nordic curl regressions, sliders, band leg curl | Nordic eccentrics are excellent but very high stimulus — start with 2x3 |
| Ab wheel | ring or TRX fallout, or a towel on a smooth floor | same anti-extension pattern |
| Parallettes | two sturdy chairs, push-up handles, dumbbells on end | stability must be verified before load |
| High bar for muscle-ups | jump-to-support on low bar; band-assisted | never train muscle-ups on a doorway bar |

## Cheapest unblocks, in order of value per euro

1. **Gymnastic rings** — turns any anchor into a full upper-body gym.
2. **Resistance bands (3 sizes)** — assistance, prehab, and load for everything.
3. **Parallettes** — wrist protection and skill progressions.
4. **Dip belt or weight vest** — the whole weighted-calisthenics progression.
5. **A single adjustable dumbbell** — unilateral leg work, rows, prehab.
6. **A doorway/portable pull-up bar only if the anchor is genuinely structural.**
