## Rules

- Never leave a mandatory field blank — write `unknown` and add it to
  `Open questions`.
- Tag every capacity value with its source: `self-reported` or
  `tested YYYY-MM-DD`. Self-reported numbers are planning estimates, not
  measurements; they get replaced after
  [assessment-testing](../../assessment-testing/SKILL.md).
- `Coach's read` is the only section where you may interpret. Everything above it
  is what the athlete said.
- Append to the changelog on every update; never rewrite history.
